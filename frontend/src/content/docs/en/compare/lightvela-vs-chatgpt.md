# LightVela vs ChatGPT: Which is best to build your personal AI assistant?

![LightVela compared with ChatGPT for building a personal AI assistant](../images/lightvela-vs-chatgpt.png)

**If your goal is to host a personal AI Agent that stays available and belongs to your own workflow, LightVela is the more direct fit.** ChatGPT is better if you want a broad, polished AI workspace for conversations, files, research, writing, and many different kinds of tasks. They overlap in what they can help you do, but they are not really the same product underneath.

## At a glance

| What matters | LightVela | ChatGPT |
| - | - | - |
| **Main job** | Host a dedicated Hermes Agent | General-purpose AI workspace |
| **Runtime** | Dedicated cloud Agent available 24/7 | ChatGPT account, chats, Projects, and GPTs |
| **Personalization** | Model, tools, persona, Skills, Channels | Memory, instructions, Projects, custom GPTs |
| **Interaction** | Messaging channels such as Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, and WeChat | ChatGPT web, mobile, desktop, voice, files, and Canvas |
| **Best fit** | A persistent Agent you configure and return to | A flexible assistant for many different tasks |

## The important distinction: workspace or Agent?

LightVela starts from a different question:

> **What if I want one actual Agent that I configure once, keep online, connect to my usual messaging apps, and build habits around?**

LightVela hosts a dedicated Hermes Agent in the cloud, with control over its model, tools, persona, Skills, and connected Channels. The Agent is designed to stay online independently of your local device.

That makes LightVela less broad, but more focused.

ChatGPT is extremely good at being the place where you ask almost anything.

OpenAI’s [ChatGPT capabilities overview](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9260256) lists web search, data analysis, voice, Canvas, memory, Projects, scheduled tasks, and custom GPTs among ChatGPT’s available capabilities, depending on your plan and settings. ChatGPT is designed as a broad conversational assistant with many tools around it.

Projects make ChatGPT especially useful for ongoing work. You can group chats, upload files, add custom instructions, and keep a project focused over time. OpenAI describes [Projects](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt) as smart workspaces for long-running efforts.

That is powerful—but it is still a workspace inside ChatGPT.

## Where LightVela is more direct

LightVela is better when the Agent itself is the product you want to keep.

You are not just opening an AI workspace for a task. You are setting up a Hermes Agent with a particular identity, model configuration, Skills, and communication channels.

The difference becomes clearer when you think in routines instead of prompts.

With ChatGPT, you might open a Project and ask it to help you plan the month.

With LightVela, you might connect Hermes to Telegram, give it a recurring workflow, and expect it to remain available tomorrow, next week, and next month—without needing your laptop to be open.

LightVela also exposes the Agent’s configuration more directly. You can choose the [model](https://lightvela.ai/docs/configuration/model), connect [Channels](https://lightvela.ai/docs/configuration), and extend Hermes through [Skills](https://lightvela.ai/docs/configuration/skill) in the configuration console.

If the Agent needs to do the same kind of work repeatedly, LightVela’s [automations](https://lightvela.ai/docs/configuration/automation) make that pattern part of the Agent’s operating routine instead of something you manually restart every time.

## Where ChatGPT is the better choice

ChatGPT is a better fit when you want a broad AI workspace without thinking about Agent setup. It can move between documents, research, images, code, and other one-off tasks as your work changes.

For ongoing specialized work, [GPTs](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554397-create-a-gpt) let you create tailored versions of ChatGPT with their own instructions and knowledge. Choose it when breadth matters more than keeping one persistent Agent identity.

## The daily-use difference

> **LightVela feels more like a personal Agent you keep. ChatGPT feels like a very capable place you go to.**

That does not mean ChatGPT cannot remember things. ChatGPT has memory, Project memory, custom instructions, and custom GPTs. The distinction is that LightVela is centered around one hosted Hermes Agent rather than a broad collection of conversations, workspaces, and assistants.

For some people, ChatGPT’s flexibility is exactly what they want. They do not want one permanent assistant identity. They want the best tool for whatever is in front of them.

For others, that flexibility can feel like constantly rebuilding the relationship: a new chat, a new Project, a new instruction block, or a new context window.
