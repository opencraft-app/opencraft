# LightVela vs Today.AI: Which is best for an always-on personal AI assistant?

## Summary

**Choose LightVela if you want a practical, always-online personal AI Agent that you can reach through messaging apps, configure with different models, extend with Skills, and schedule with Scheduled Tasks.** Choose Today.AI if you want a more app-centric personal operating system built around your daily data and proactive suggestions.

LightVela’s documented product model centers on a cloud-hosted Hermes Agent, while Today.AI’s public positioning emphasizes personal context, proactive help, and cross-device assistance.
LightVela is the more immediately understandable choice for persistent Agent hosting and explicit control.
Today.AI represents the more experimental vision: an assistant that builds a living picture of your life and acts before you ask.

## At a glance

This is a comparison of product shape and everyday ownership, not a benchmark or pricing verdict.
The public information available for Today.AI is still more limited than the product documentation LightVela publishes.

| What matters | LightVela | Today.AI |
| - | - | - |
| **Product shape** | Cloud-hosted Hermes Agent | Personal AI assistant and AI-native OS vision |
| **Main access model** | Messaging Channels plus web console | Native app and cross-device experience |
| **Memory** | Persistent Agent memory across conversations | Living memory built from personal context |
| **Proactive work** | Explicit Scheduled Tasks and scheduled jobs | Designed to act before the user asks |
| **Extensibility** | Models, Channels, Skills, and Scheduled Tasks | Model and ecosystem details still developing |
| **Best fit** | Users who want a usable always-on Agent now | Early adopters seeking a proactive personal OS |

## What changes in everyday use

The simplest difference is where the assistant lives.
LightVela puts Hermes Agent in the cloud, then lets you reach that same Agent through Channels such as Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, or WeChat.
Today.AI is presented more like a personal operating layer: it collects context from your devices and services, organizes what matters, and turns that context into timely suggestions.

Imagine a Monday morning.
Today.AI’s public [landing page](https://today.ai/landing) shows an intended experience built around a morning brief that connects your schedule, health signals, projects, relationships, and unfinished goals.
It also presents living memory, proactive suggestions, app connectors, cloud computers, and cross-device control as parts of the same assistant experience.

With LightVela, you define the assistant’s working environment more explicitly.
You connect a model, choose a Channel, install useful [Skills](../configuration/skill.md), and create scheduled jobs such as reminders, digests, or recurring research through [Scheduled Tasks](../configuration/automation.md).
The result feels less like an operating system that observes your entire day and more like a dependable Agent that you deliberately shape.

That difference affects trust and predictability.
LightVela asks you to decide which capabilities and schedules should exist.
Today.AI’s promise is that broader context lets it notice what may matter next and intervene at the right moment.
One approach gives you a clear control surface.
The other aims for a more ambient assistant that becomes useful by understanding more of your life.

## Where LightVela is the better fit

LightVela’s strongest advantage is operational clarity.
It is designed to remove the infrastructure work behind a persistent Agent: no server setup, container maintenance, or local computer that must remain awake.
The [LightVela overview](../01-overview.md) documents a cloud-hosted Hermes Agent that stays available independently of your personal device.

That matters because memory and proactive work only become useful when the Agent stays available.
A laptop-based assistant may be powerful during an active session, but it becomes unavailable when the machine sleeps, changes networks, or is replaced.
LightVela keeps the Agent running independently, so scheduled tasks and future conversations do not depend on whether your personal device is online.

LightVela is also clearer about configuration boundaries.
Its model separates the main building blocks into Hermes Agent, models, Channels, Skills, and Scheduled Tasks.
Multiple Channels can connect to the same Agent and share its memory and behavior, while Skills add reusable capabilities for specific workflows.

This gives you a useful kind of control.
You can change the model without rebuilding the Agent, add a Channel without creating a second personality, and install a Skill when you need a repeatable workflow.
Scheduled Tasks then provide the scheduling layer for reminders, summaries, notifications, and other recurring tasks.

LightVela is particularly attractive if your AI already belongs in your communication tools.
Instead of opening another standalone dashboard, you can ask the same Agent a question in Telegram, continue on another connected Channel, and keep the conversation attached to one persistent identity and memory.

## Where Today.AI is the better fit

Today.AI’s strongest case is its product ambition.
Its public positioning describes an assistant centered on the individual, with living memory, proactive help, a growing connector ecosystem, cloud computers, cross-device control, and community Skills.

That is a different starting point from LightVela.
Today.AI is trying to make the assistant understand the shape of your day before you formulate a request.
The experience may be compelling if you want personal context to connect your schedule, projects, health signals, relationships, and unfinished goals into one daily information stream.

Today.AI is also interesting for people who prefer native applications and cross-device help.
Its public materials describe an experience that can work across the devices you already use, while cloud computers handle browsers, files, and code when a task needs more than a suggestion.
Community Skills are presented as another way to bring reusable workflows into the assistant.

The important qualification is maturity and visibility.
Today.AI’s public homepage currently directs visitors to a waitlist, and its Terms describe the service as an early-access beta whose features may change.
Its specific model and ecosystem details are therefore still developing.
This is an evolving product rather than a fully transparent, broadly available platform.

That makes Today.AI a natural choice for early adopters who want to explore the idea of a personal operating layer.
It is a less obvious choice if you need documented setup paths, predictable configuration boundaries, and a working cloud-hosted Agent you can start using now.

## Which should you choose?

Choose LightVela if you:

- **Want an always-on Agent:** You want an AI Agent that does not depend on your laptop or phone staying awake.
- **Prefer familiar access:** You want to use messaging Channels instead of relying on one new standalone app.
- **Want explicit control:** You want to configure models, Skills, Channels, memory, and scheduled Scheduled Tasks.
- **Value documented setup:** You want configuration paths that you can understand, repeat, and adjust.
- **Want to start now:** You prefer a working cloud-hosted Hermes Agent while the product grows with you.

Choose Today.AI if you:

- **Want broader personal context:** You want the assistant to build a living picture from your data, devices, and services.
- **Prefer native apps:** You want a Mac, iOS, or Android-oriented experience across devices.
- **Want proactive suggestions:** You are excited by daily information streams and help that arrives before you ask.
- **Accept wider data access:** You are comfortable authorizing personal context as part of the assistant experience.
- **Enjoy early access:** You are willing to join an evolving product while its models and ecosystem become clearer.

My take: **LightVela is better suited to people who want a persistent personal Agent they can configure and use today. Today.AI is better suited to early adopters who want to explore what happens when an AI assistant becomes a personal operating layer.**

Neither product needs to be dismissed.
They are solving adjacent problems: LightVela makes a capable Agent dependable, reachable, and extensible, while Today.AI is exploring how deeply an assistant can understand and anticipate the rhythm of one person’s life.
