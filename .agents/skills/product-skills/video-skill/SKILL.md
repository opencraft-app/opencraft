# Video Script Generator

Create spoken LightVela videos inspired by the provided Lovable examples.
Match their pacing and factual progression without copying wording.

## Video contract

| Section | Timing | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Topic story | 00:00-00:32 | Spend two-thirds on an unrelated factual mini-story. Do not mention LightVela, the creator, or the prompt. |
| Product story | 00:32-00:48 | Spend one-third turning the analogy into a concrete LightVela automation, automatic result, and CTA. |

Use `LightVela` by default.
If the brief names Owner or Guozi, preserve the name exactly and use first person naturally.

The product story must show an AI automation, not a one-off build.
Give it a schedule or trigger, repeatable instructions, and a useful output delivered automatically.
Prefer safe research, monitoring, summaries, or content workflows, and never invent integrations.

## Topic progression

Everything before the first LightVela mention belongs to the topic story.
Build a progression, not a list of similar statistics.
Facts include manufacturing, machinery, production capacity, origins, craft difficulty, and process changes, not only numbers.

Use four beats in order:

1. **Scale hook:** Give one headline fact followed by exactly three extraordinary comparisons in one burst.
Use three different lenses, such as area, landmark height, filled spaces, weight, object count, or people fed.
Each comparison must amplify the scale and be surprising on its own.
Do not use a plain per-person average or another normalization that makes the headline feel smaller.
2. **Curiosity bridge:** Ask the question raised by that scale, such as “So how do we make so much?”
3. **Production answer:** Give verified details about how it is made, including a machine, rate, material, or process step.
4. **Transformation:** Contrast an older skilled, slow, or manual method with the modern process.

Every beat must change the dimension: **scale → question → production → transformation**.
One headline fact may power several distinct opening comparisons, but do not repeat it later.
Never pad with annual, seasonal, holiday, and per-second versions of the same consumption fact.
If more words are needed, research production, history, mechanism, or craft.

State only the comparison result in spoken copy; keep measurement setup and assumptions in `Fact note`.
Prefer outrageous named structures or quantities over ordinary distances.
Use each key number and named comparison once.
Literal machinery such as a real conveyor belt is useful; generic “picture,” “imagine,” or reaction filler is not.

## Idea Mode

Generate eight evergreen, trend-researched, or mixed ideas, defaulting to mixed.
For current trends, browse Google Trends and TikTok Creative Center, then verify every script fact on the web.
Do not call an idea trending without a current source.
Avoid tragedy, politics, medical claims, and unverified numbers.

```text
1. Topic: <topic>
   Headline fact: <main scale fact>
   Comparisons: <exactly three amplifying equivalences using different lenses>
   Curiosity bridge: <question>
   Production fact: <machine, rate, material, or process>
   Transformation: <old method → modern method>
   Product hook: <analogous recurring LightVela challenge>
   Automation prompt: <schedule, repeatable workflow, and delivered output>
   Research: <source URLs>
```

## Script Mode

Write 105-120 spoken words for about 48 seconds.
Use short, energetic, natural sentences.
Do not invent facts or product capabilities.

```text
Title: <short clickbait title>

00:00-00:12  <headline fact plus exactly three varied comparisons>
00:12-00:15  <curiosity bridge>
00:15-00:24  <production answer>
00:24-00:32  <old method → modern transformation>
00:32-00:36  <analogy and clickbaity LightVela automation hook>
00:36-00:44  <plain-English schedule plus repeatable task>
00:44-00:48  <automatic delivered result and short CTA>

Estimated runtime: <seconds>
Automation prompt: <exact recurring-task prompt>
Fact note: <sources and calculation assumptions>
```

Final check: the topic changes dimension at every beat, uses varied comparisons, includes real production facts, repeats no key fact or named comparison, and ends with a genuine LightVela automation.
