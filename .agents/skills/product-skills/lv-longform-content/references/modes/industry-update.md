# Industry update

Use for a concrete model release, product update, hardware trend, research result, policy event, or ecosystem shift. Keep it topic-first, observational, and evidence-led—not a tutorial or product ad.

Suggested arc: title → one generated hero → answer-first summary → short version → what happened → evidence/data → what changed and what did not → caveats → who should care → industry meaning → personal take and what I’m watching next → concise LightVela bridge → sources.

## Evidence

- Research before outlining. Prefer official announcements/docs, papers and research institutions, regulators/government, original benchmarks/datasets, filings, or formal interviews. Use reputable edited media for context and independent perspective.
- Cross-check material facts, dates, prices, performance figures, and policy claims with two independent sources where possible. State scope, units, dates, sample/benchmark limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty.
- Keep traceable inline links or a concise `Sources` section. Do not treat social posts, SEO farms, reposts, affiliate roundups, or screenshots as sole evidence for a material claim.
- Use a Markdown table only when it makes data, a timeline, specifications, comparison, or sources clearer.

## One generated hero

Call Codex `imagegen` to make exactly one original 16:9 hero image. Do not output `[RECOMMEND ADD IMAGE: ...]` or request other article images.

```text
Create one 16:9 editorial hero image for an industry-update article about "<specific topic>".
Use an exceptionally clean, minimal futuristic blog-poster aesthetic: soft lavender and pale violet gradients, glossy studio lighting, restrained glass or metallic materials, generous negative space, minimal UI-like accents, and a few carefully placed topic-relevant floating elements.
Make the main subject unmistakably about <specific product, model, hardware, research, event, or policy>. Use one clear visual metaphor for the article’s actual change or tension—not a generic humanoid robot.
Place the main subject on the right or lower-right; preserve the left or upper-left for a short, readable 2–5 word headline and optional small "INDUSTRY UPDATE" eyebrow. Use sparse modern sans-serif type only; no paragraphs, fake UI copy, dense labels, fake logos, brand marks, or unreadable microtext. Match the article language.
Keep it polished, editorial, and topic-specific. Avoid clutter, excessive neon, stock-photo realism, and visual noise.
```

## LightVela boundary

Hermes and LightVela are optional context, not the subject. Use one concise, natural LightVela bridge near the end only; this is the only place LightVela links may appear. Verify its route, language-localization, and attribution with `lv-seo-geo`. Do not add a separate “Closing Thought” section—merge judgment and future monitoring into `Personal Take and What I’m Watching Next`.
