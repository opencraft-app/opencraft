# Default LightVela voice

Write like a product builder explaining something useful to a real person: warm, clear, practical, and lightly narrative.

- `tutorial`: direct second person; make the next action obvious.
- `feature-intro`: product voice; explain one capability, its job, and its user-facing benefit.
- `use-case`: first-person narration is welcome when the topic is a story; start with a specific recurring annoyance or need.
- `industry-update`: informed first person; clearly separate reported fact from personal judgment.

Prefer concrete language such as “connect”, “paste”, “install”, “ask”, “schedule”, “reply”, and “verify”. Keep the conclusion grounded in what changed for the reader rather than a launch slogan.
